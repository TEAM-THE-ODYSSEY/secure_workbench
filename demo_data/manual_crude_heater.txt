1. OVERVIEW
Furnace F-101 heats desaltered crude oil from 210 °C to the flash zone temperature of 350 °C
before entry into flash zone of Column C-101.

2. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
- Design Duty: 120 GCal/hr
- Burners: 24 natural draft floor burners operating on Refinery Fuel Gas (RFG).
- Tube Metallurgy: 9Cr-1Mo seamless alloy steel tubes.
- Skin Temperature Limit: Maximum allowable tube skin temperature is 650 °C.

3. COMPRESSOR K-201 (HYDROCRACKER UNIT HCU)
Reciprocating compressor K-201 handles high-pressure recycle hydrogen gas in HCU.
- Suction Pressure: 140.0 bar
- Discharge Pressure: 175.0 bar
- Lubrication: Forced feed synthetic ISO VG 150 oil.
- Interstage cooling is maintained via Heat Exchanger E-205.

4. MAINTENANCE & INSPECTION FREQUENCY
- Burner tip cleaning: Every 90 days or if flame impingement is observed on tubes.
- Tube thickness measurement (UT gauging): Conducted annually during scheduled turnaround.
- Refer to SOP-CDU-101 for feed pump operation feeding Furnace F-101.
